# Project brief

## Main research question
How are FTSE 100 companies using generative AI?

## Suggested subquestions
1. Which business functions contain the most reported use cases?
2. What proportion of disclosures describe live deployments, pilots, planned uses or broad ambitions?
3. How does adoption differ by sector?
4. Are companies primarily using generative AI internally, for customers, or in products sold to clients?
5. Which model providers and technology partners are most frequently named?
6. What benefits are claimed, and how often are those benefits supported by measurable evidence?
7. What risks or governance controls are disclosed alongside the use case?

## Population and date rule
Population: companies listed in the FTSE 100 following the 19 June 2026 changes.

Evidence window: 1 January 2023 to 14 July 2026.

The index membership date and evidence window must be stated in the final report because FTSE 100 membership changes over time.

## Recommended scope
A complete 100-company census is possible but document collection is the largest workload. For an initial portfolio version, use a stratified sample of 30 companies across sectors. Expand to all 100 only after testing the coding process.

## Unit of analysis
One distinct publicly reported generative-AI use case.

A use case is distinct when it has a different task, user group, business process, product or deployment context. Multiple sources describing the same use case should be linked rather than counted repeatedly.

## Inclusion criteria
Include a record when:
- the company or a named partner explicitly identifies generative AI, an LLM, a foundation model or a named generative-AI product; and
- a task, process, product, experiment or intended application is described.

## Exclusion criteria
Exclude:
- vague statements that AI is important;
- predictive AI or conventional machine learning with no generative component;
- general employee access to a tool where no use or task is described;
- third-party speculation;
- duplicate reporting of the same use case;
- evidence published outside the date window.

## Primary analytical categories
- Customer service
- Software development and IT
- Marketing and content
- Knowledge search and document work
- Operations and supply chain
- Risk, legal and compliance
- Finance and administration
- Human resources
- Product or service innovation
- Research and development
- Sales
- Other

## Final portfolio outputs
1. Clean CSV dataset with citations and quotations.
2. Charts comparing sectors, functions and deployment stages.
3. A concise research report explaining findings and limitations.
4. A reproducible GitHub repository.
5. A manual validation exercise showing where the LLM made errors.
